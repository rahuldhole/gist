How Percentage Formulas Work

Percentage of a Number

Convert the percentage to a decimal by dividing by 100, then multiply by the total value. Example: (20 / 100) × 150 = 30.

Percentage Change

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the absolute old value, and multiply by 100. Example: ((135 - 100) / 100) × 100 = +35%.
