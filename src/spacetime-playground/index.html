How to read this

Gravity isn't pulling you toward the ground

Open on YouTube

In general relativity, Earth's mass slows down clocks near its surface. Your head ages slightly faster than your feet.

Because you are always travelling through time, this gradient causes your path in 4D spacetime to pivot toward the slower region — which we experience as the downward acceleration of gravity!
